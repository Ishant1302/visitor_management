HOW TO OPEN YOUR WEBSITE BY CLICKING:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TO START THE WEBSITE & OPEN YOUR BROWSER:
   Double-click:  "CLICK_TO_START_WEBSITE.bat"
   - This automatically starts both the Backend (port 3001) and Frontend (port 5173).
   - It will automatically open your default browser to: http://localhost:5173
   - Keep the black command window open while using the website.

2. IF THE SERVER IS ALREADY RUNNING:
   Double-click:  "CLICK_TO_OPEN_WEBSITE.url"
   - This directly opens your browser to the website without opening any terminal.

3. TO PUT SHORTCUTS ON YOUR DESKTOP:
   Double-click:  "CREATE_DESKTOP_SHORTCUT.bat"
   - This puts direct 1-click icons onto your Windows Desktop!

4. TO STOP THE SERVERS:
   Double-click:  "STOP_WEBSITE.bat"
   - Closes background processes and frees ports 5173 and 3001.
